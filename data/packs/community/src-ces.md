# CES — Cooperative Election Study (formerly CCES)

A large annual respondent-level survey of US political attitudes and
behaviour (~50-60k+/year), openly downloadable from Harvard Dataverse.



Pick the right file — Common Content vs team modules vs the cumulative
file are different products. Weight variable names differ by year.
