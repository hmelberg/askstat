# College Scorecard

Institution-year and field-of-study-year US postsecondary outcomes: cost,
admissions, completion, post-completion earnings (1/4/10yr by field) and
debt/repayment — largely an IRS/NSLDS administrative match. Open, free
key.

```yaml
- id: src-college-scorecard
  name: College Scorecard
  unit: institution-year and field-of-study-year
  content: "cost, admissions, completion, post-completion earnings 1/4/10yr by field, debt/repayment — largely IRS/NSLDS administrative match"
  access: open
  api: "https://api.data.gov/ed/collegescorecard/v1/schools — free key"
```

Query `https://api.data.gov/ed/collegescorecard/v1/schools` with a free
api.data.gov key. See also the Urban Institute Education Data Portal (id:
src-urban-education-data), which unifies College Scorecard alongside
IPEDS and other sources.
