# EU-EFIGE / Bruegel-UniCredit dataset

Openly downloadable manufacturing-firm survey covering seven European
countries — a single wave, not a panel.

```yaml
- id: src-eu-efige
  name: EU-EFIGE / Bruegel-UniCredit dataset
  unit: manufacturing firm, [AT, FR, DE, HU, IT, ES, UK]
  access: open — https://www.bruegel.org/dataset/efige
  gotcha: "single survey wave (~2008-2010), not a panel on the survey side; stratified by size/country so weights required; manufacturing only"
```

A single survey wave (~2008-2010), not a panel on the survey side;
stratified by size/country so weights are required; manufacturing firms
only.
