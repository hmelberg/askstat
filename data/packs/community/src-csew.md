# CSEW — Crime Survey for England and Wales

A household/person/incident-level victimization survey, free registration
via UK Data Service (Special Licence needed for finer geography).

Fraud and computer misuse offences only entered the headline series from
~2017 — a real break in comparability with earlier years.
