# CPS Outgoing Rotation Group (CPS-ORG)

Individual worker-month microdata from the Current Population Survey's
Outgoing Rotation Group, distributed as open CEPR uniform extracts — a
distinct, ready-to-use extract path rather than the raw Census CPS
microdata. Updated monthly since 1979.

```yaml
- id: src-cps-org
  name: CPS Outgoing Rotation Group
  unit: individual worker-month
  access: open — CEPR uniform extracts, updated monthly, 1979-present
  gotcha: "earnings top-coded; occupation/industry coding changed 2002/03 and again 2020"
```

Earnings are top-coded, and occupation/industry coding changed in 2002/03
and again in 2020 — do not pool coded categories across those breaks
without a crosswalk.
