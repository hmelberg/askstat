# Human Mortality Database (HMD)

Aggregate period/cohort life tables, death counts, exposure and births —
not individual records. Free registration; ~41 countries, with Sweden's
series back to 1751 and most others from the early 1900s.



Login moved in ~2022 to mortality.org/Account/Auth — any pre-2022 script
will fail; use HMDHFDplus >= 2.x.
