# NHTS — National Household Travel Survey

A household/person/vehicle/trip-level US travel survey with state
add-ons, openly downloadable.



Use jackknife replicate weights for variance estimation. The 2022
NextGen GPS-assisted redesign is not fully comparable with earlier
waves — do not pool naively across that break.
