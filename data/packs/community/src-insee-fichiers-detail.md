# France — INSEE census "fichiers détail"

True microdata — individual and household extracts from France's rolling
annual census, aggregated to region/department/canton/city geography.
Open, freely downloadable from insee.fr.



Weight with IPONDI. Counts below ~200 are flagged imprecise, and zones
under 2,000 inhabitants are excluded from complementary tables.
