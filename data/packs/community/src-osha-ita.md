# OSHA Injury Tracking Application (ITA)

Open establishment-level workplace injury/illness data — Form 300A annual
summaries plus case-level Forms 300/301 — 2016-present.



Only establishments above size/industry thresholds report. There is no
stable establishment ID across years — name/address matching is needed to
link an establishment over time.
