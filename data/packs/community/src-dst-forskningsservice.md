# Danmarks Statistik — Forskningsservice

Genuine microdata access inside a controlled environment
(Forskermaskinen) — richer than microdata.no's Norwegian
anonymised-output-only model. Foreign researchers need collaboration with
or sponsorship by a Danish research institution.
