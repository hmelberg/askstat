# ECB Survey on the Access to Finance of Enterprises (SAFE)

SME/firm-level survey on access to finance, semi-annual/annual since
2009. Free registration.



It is a repeated cross-section with rotation, not a true firm panel —
don't treat consecutive waves as the same firms.
