# MEDSL — MIT Election Data and Science Lab precinct returns

Precinct x office x candidate x year US election returns, openly
downloadable from Harvard Dataverse.

```yaml
- id: src-medsl
  name: MIT Election Data and Science Lab precinct returns
  unit: precinct × office × candidate × year
  access: OPENLY DOWNLOADABLE (Harvard Dataverse)
  gotcha: "precinct boundaries change — match to the election-year vintage of the boundary shapefile"
```

Precinct boundaries change over time — match returns to the
election-year vintage of the boundary shapefile, not a current-day one.
