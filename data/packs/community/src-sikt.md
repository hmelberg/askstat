# Sikt (formerly NSD) — Forskningsdatabanken / Surveybanken

Freely downloadable survey data from recurring Norwegian surveys, SSB,
research institutes and individual deposits, much of it without
application. Also hosts and distributes the European Social Survey (ESS).



See the europe-surveys pack for ESS (European Social Survey) detail.
