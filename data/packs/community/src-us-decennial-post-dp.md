# US decennial census — post-differential-privacy microdata

The traditional decennial PUMS was already replaced pre-2020 by the ACS;
Census's stated successor is a Privacy-Protected Microdata File (PPMF,
released 5 Aug 2024) — individual-level, differentially private,
block-level custom geography.



PPMF access tier is UNVERIFIED — likely restricted/approved-researcher,
not a simple open download. Small-area decennial counts have documented
accuracy degradation vs pre-2020 products.
