# FJC IDB — Federal Judicial Center Integrated Database

Docket-level federal court case data (civil, criminal, bankruptcy,
appellate) — every federal court case since 1970, fully open.



Nature-of-suit codes changed over the decades — use the FJC Research
Guide crosswalks rather than comparing raw codes across eras. This is
docket-level metadata only, with no document text.
