# Administrative Data Research UK (ADR UK)

A UK-wide initiative for linking administrative and survey data for
research access. It brands itself as one programme, but access actually
routes through four separate governance regimes depending on which nation's
data is needed — there is no single unified application.



Identify which nation's data is needed first, then apply through that
specific gate: ONS SRS for ONS-held data under a sharing agreement, NISRA
for Northern Ireland government and health data, eDRIS for Scotland, or the
SAIL Databank for Wales (billions of anonymised person-records).
