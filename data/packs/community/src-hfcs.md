# ECB Household Finance and Consumption Survey (HFCS)

True microdata on household wealth, debt, income and consumption across
the euro area, with waves in 2010, 2014, 2017 and 2021 — unit is the
household, with individual-level labour/income detail per member. Access
requires an application (government photo ID, English CV, request form
to hfcs.access@ecb.europa.eu).



Each country-wave ships 5 multiply-imputed implicates — variance
estimation needs Rubin's rules, and several countries oversample the
wealthy (ES, FR).
